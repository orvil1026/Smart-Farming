Why is it necessary to detect pest in plant ?

Detecting pests in plants is essential for several reasons: Pests can cause severe damage to crops, leading to a reduction in yield and quality. Identifying and controlling pests early can prevent crop damage and ensure a successful harvest. Pest damage can result in economic losses for farmers and the agricultural industry. By detecting pests early and implementing effective control measures, losses can be minimized.
